Love You Better support

Love You Better sends one greeting card a day from one person to another. Here is help with the things people ask about most.

Getting in touch

Open an issue on the support page on GitHub. Anything you post there is public, so do not include your cards, replies, names or other personal details. Describe what you saw and what you expected.

Pairing

How does the person receiving cards connect?

The sender sets up the app first and gets a pairing card with a code. The receiver installs Love You Better, opens the camera on their iPhone and scans the code. The app opens and connects. No account or password is needed.

The code says it is closed or cannot be opened

A pairing code works for one person. If it was already used, or the sender made a new code, the old one stops working. Ask the sender for a new code from Settings, then Pairing.

I deleted the app or got a new phone

Install the app again and tap "I got a card". If you were connected before, your cards come back without a new code. Sign in to the same Apple Account first.

Cards

My card has not arrived

A card opens on its own date, by the date on the iPhone. Check that you are signed in to iCloud, that you have a connection, and that Date and Time is set to "Set Automatically" in Settings, General.

The app says my iPhone's date looks wrong

Cards open by the real date, not a date set by hand. In Settings, General, Date and Time, turn on "Set Automatically".

A card says "Getting your card" or "Still arriving"

Artwork and photos download when they are needed. Stay connected for a moment. Today's card and any waiting cards download first.

Notifications

I am not getting a daily reminder

Open Settings on your iPhone, then Notifications, then Love You Better, and turn on Allow Notifications. In the app, open Settings and check that Daily reminder is on and that the reminder time is what you want. Reminder text never shows the card itself.

iCloud

The app says iCloud is signed out

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap Sign in at the top, and use the Apple Account you used before. Your cards are in iCloud and will come back.

The sender's iCloud storage is full

Cards, photos, videos and replies count against the sender's iCloud storage. The app shows a note when storage is full. Free up space or choose a larger iCloud plan, then tap Try again.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. The short version: your cards live in your own iCloud, and the developer cannot see them.
