Love You Better privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Love You Better is a private app for sending one greeting card a day from one person to another. The developer does not collect, receive or see any of your information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Where your information lives

Everything you write, send or save in Love You Better is stored in iCloud, the service built into your iPhone. The developer runs no server and has no access to it.

  • The person who writes the cards (the sender) keeps the cards, their designs, photos, videos, voice memos, replies and reactions in their own private iCloud storage. This counts against their iCloud storage.
  • The person who receives the cards shares that storage through a private two-person share that the sender sets up with a pairing code. Only those two Apple Accounts can use it.
  • The receiver's favorites are kept in the receiver's own private iCloud storage.
  • Card messages, replies and other personal text are stored in encrypted iCloud fields.

Apple provides and protects iCloud. Their privacy policy applies to it: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps a copy of your cards and a small set of settings on your iPhone so it works without a connection. Notifications are planned on your phone and carry no card or reply text. Face ID and your passcode are handled by iOS. The app never receives your face data or passcode.

What the app asks permission for

  • Notifications: a daily reminder and a few other alerts. You can turn each type off in the app.
  • Camera and microphone: only when the sender adds a photo, a video or a voice memo to a card.
  • Photos: add only. The app can save a card or a photo you choose to your photo library. It cannot read your library. The photo picker used to choose photos for a card needs no permission.
  • Apple Music: to search for a song and play it inside a card. This uses your own Apple Music subscription and is handled by Apple.
  • Face ID: to lock the app, if you keep that setting on.

Links in a card

When the sender adds a link to a card, or imports cards that contain links, the sender's iPhone asks that website for its page title. The website sees an ordinary request from the sender's phone. Nothing goes through the developer.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app contains no third-party code, no analytics and no advertising. It does not track you across other apps or websites. For this reason the App Store privacy label for Love You Better reads "Data Not Collected".

Children

Love You Better is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your information

  • The sender can delete a card that has not been opened yet, and either person can delete their own replies. Deleted items are removed from iCloud.
  • The sender can remove the receiver from the share in the app. The receiver's phone then clears its copy.
  • Deleting the app removes the copy on your phone. To remove what is stored in iCloud, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, choose Love You Better and delete its data.
